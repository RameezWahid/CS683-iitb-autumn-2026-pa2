  dyn(nopf) Dynamic I-RRIP without any prefetcher (Task 2B run)

  vA  prefetch fill RRPV 3, prefetch hit: no promotion
  vB  prefetch fill RRPV 2, prefetch hit: no promotion
  vC  prefetch fill RRPV 3, prefetch hit: normal promotion
  vD  adaptive: RRPV 2 if >=25% of the last 1024 L2 prefetch fills got a demand hit in the L2, else RRPV 3
  vE  prefetch fill RRPV 0, prefetch hit: no promotion
  vF  prefetch fill RRPV 1, prefetch hit: no promotion
  vG  prefetch fill RRPV 2, no promotion, unused prefetched lines at RRPV 3 evicted first
  vH  prefetch fill RRPV 1, prefetch hit: normal promotion           <== final design
  vH2 vH re-run with a rebuilt binary (determinism check, identical)
  vI  as vF, but leader sets ignore prefetches (plain SRRIP insertion for them)
  vJ  as vH, and in instruction mode unused prefetched lines are never chosen while other data exists
  vK  prefetch fill RRPV 0, prefetch hit: normal promotion
  vL  as vH, but RRPV 3 whenever the L1D prefetch accuracy of the last 1024 L2 prefetch fills is < 40%
  vM  as vH, and writeback fills inserted at RRPV 3 (not a prefetch idea; checked for completeness)
  vN  as vH with threshold 0.10

not the policy (the final file uses pf_used and reports the correct value, e.g. 981 instead of
1962 for vH on trace1).
